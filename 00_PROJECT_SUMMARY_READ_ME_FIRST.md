## 📈 Key Findings Summary

### The Problem
- **60.6% churn rate** on Day 1 (18,180 users/month lost)
- **3.8 app store rating** (lowest among competitors)
- **$2.6M/year wasted CAC**
- **Three critical problems:** Free content undiscoverable, hidden paywalls, save failures

### The Root Cause
- **No team owns end-to-end activation**
- Organizational incentive misalignment
- Each team optimizes local metrics, activation falls through cracks

### The Solution
**MVP (3 weeks, $25K):**
- 7 features targeting discovery + trust
- Expected: 39.4% → 62.1% activation (+22.7 pts)
- Revenue: +$624K annually
- Risk: LOW (all UI/UX changes)

**Phase 2 (6 weeks, $100K):**
- Save infrastructure overhaul
- Expected: 62.1% → 72.3% activation (+10.2 pts)
- Revenue: +$235K additional annually
- Risk: MEDIUM (managed rollout)

### The Impact
- **Total activation improvement:** +32.9 percentage points
- **Additional users:** +9,318/month (+78.9% increase)
- **Annual revenue:** +$859K
- **ROI:** 587% in Year 1
- **Payback period:** 1.7 months

### The Recommendation
**✅ APPROVE MVP implementation immediately**
- High confidence (80% probability >60% activation)
- Low risk (all reversible)
- Fast time-to-value (3 weeks)
- Strong ROI (2,496% on MVP alone)
